Contemporary self-catering accommodation in peaceful green surroundings

The Palm Seychelles is a residence of luxury apartments providing beautiful nature and Indian Ocean views in Mahe. We are near the white sandy beach of Beau Vallon ideal for swimming and snorkelling. Designed as a true home away from home, our property with outdoor pool offers tailored services and optimal comfort. Set in a calm and peaceful environment, these unique apartments and exclusive private villa are perfect for a tropical island holiday, a honeymoon, a special celebration, or a business stay in the Seychelles archipelago.
