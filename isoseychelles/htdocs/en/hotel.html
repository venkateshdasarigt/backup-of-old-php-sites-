Location

The Palm Seychelles offers a quiet, secluded location at Mount Simpson, on the north-west coast of the island of Mahe. Conveniently located in the district of Bel Ombre, just 10 minutes walk from Beau Vallon Beach, these unique apartments sit in peaceful, relaxing surroundings.

Read more Less

Providing the perfect setting between the Indian Ocean and the Morne Seychellois National Park, the Palm Seychelles provides the ideal base to explore Mahe and the surrounding islands. Victoria, the Seychelles main town, is a mere 15 minutes drive away. There is a public bus stop near the hotel with regular service. Should you wish to be independent we recommend you hire a car to get round the island easily, we have a free private car park available.

Reception

Enjoy a warm welcome and world class service during your stay at The Palm Seychelles. We have an extended knowledge of the island and look forward to sharing our best addresses with you. A complete tour service is available to help you make the most of the beautiful Seychelles archipelago. We can call a taxi, book a restaurant for you and organize all your tours and excursions.

Food & Beverage

For an even more relaxing holiday, let our chef take care of your meals.

We provide a private catering service on request. Discover the best of Creole cuisine with home-cooked specialities to be enjoyed in your own apartment. During the day refreshing tropical fruit are served by the swimming pool.

Breakfast

Each morning we serve a continental breakfast by the pool or in our patio overlooking a refreshing mountain spring. It comprises bread with butter/jam/honey, a selection of cheeses, croissants, pastries, rolls, fruit juices and various hot beverages. Eggs cooked to order complete the offer.
