Location

It’s conveniently located on the North-West Coast of the main island of Mahé. The famous Beau-Vallon beach is a mere 5 minutes leisurely walk away.

You are never too far from the various restaurants, discotheque, night club, casino, hotels, shops and supermarkets.

The capital city of Victoria is only 15 min drive away.

There is a nearby public bus stop; buses operate on a half-hour schedule.
