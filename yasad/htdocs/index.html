DISCOVER PRASLIN

Praslin is Seychelles' second largest island and has a population of approximately 7,000 people.

The island is a popular destination for tourists as it is home to some of the world's most exquisite beaches such as Anse Lazio and Anse Georgette.

At the centre of Praslin lies the Vallee de Mai nature reserve. The Valle is host to six species of endemic palms which can only be found in Seychelles.

VALLEE DE MAI

The legendary Vallee de Mai is one of two UNESCO World Heritage sites in Seychelles and was once believed to be the original Garden of Eden.

It is the largest intact forest of the endemic Coco de Mer palm.

Follow the trails through this unique palm forest to discover the ancient secrets it holds.

ANSE LAZIO

Anse Lazio is the most famous beach on Praslin and is often listed among the world's top 10 beaches.

Beautifully framed by granite rocks, this dream beach has soft white sands which lead gently down to the calm clear waters that are perfect for swimming.

FISHING

Whether you are an experienced sports fisherman or an enthusiastic beginner, you will be thrilled by the opportunities that Seychelles' fish-rich waters offer.

Try your hand at big-game fishing and the unforgettable experience of trolling in pristine azure waters.

You may also get the chance to try the traditional method of bottom-fishing where you can catch some of the finest tasting fish on the planet.

DIVING

One of Seychelles' best kept secrets is the many opportunities it offers for world-class diving on both granite and coral reefs teeming with shoals of coral fish in warm, crystal waters still untouched by man.

The Inner Islands feature an abundance of fish, even on shallow inshore reefs.
